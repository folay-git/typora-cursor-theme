## Typora's GitHub theme

`cursor-style/base.css` is built on `github.css`, the "GitHub" theme that ships with Typora. The document structure, block geometry and editing-related rules of that theme are kept; the colours, type hierarchy, spacing and display rules are rewritten. `github.css` itself is not part of this package. It is distributed with Typora without a separate licence text, and its copyright stays with Typora and the authors of that theme. The MIT licence does not extend to the parts derived from `github.css`.
